! =====================================================================
! Video ads: Google IMA SDK and DoubleClick video media — all sites
! Verified: 7 October 2026 (playback tested, see below)
! =====================================================================

! These hosts serve video ads (pre-rolls) in news sites' own players,
! not ordinary page ads. They are kept separate on purpose: no
! upstream list blocks them at DNS level, because a DNS rule can't make
! per-site exceptions and some video players stall when the ad SDK is
! missing. If a video player anywhere on the network breaks, comment
! out the rules in this section first.
!
! EasyList blocks ||imasdk.googleapis.com^ but allows ima3.js again on
! ~90 sites (Plex, Pluto TV, Crunchyroll, BBC, screen9 and more) where
! players need it. It blocks ||2mdn.net^$~media, i.e. everything but
! the video files; a DNS rule can't make that distinction, so these
! rules also stop the ad video files themselves.
!
! Tested 2026-10-07 in headless Chromium, today's rules vs. today's
! rules plus this section:
! - DN (screen9 player, loads ima3.js): videos played identically.
! - Aftonbladet TV (tv.aftonbladet.se): played identically; no IMA
!   requests at all once the rest of this list is applied.
! - YouTube (watch pages and embeds) never requests these hosts and
!   played identically, its own pre-roll ad included; YouTube's ads
!   come from youtube.com/googlevideo.com.
! Not tested: smart-TV/streaming apps on the network (TV4 Play, Plex,
! Pluto TV, ...), which may use the IMA SDK for their ads.
